# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

xCAST maintainers and contributors are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project environment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in language, tone, and feedback.
- Assume good intent, but prioritize clarity and evidence.
- Give actionable technical feedback, not personal criticism.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Focus discussions on the code, architecture, and project outcomes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, intimidation, or threats.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after feedback from maintainers.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained off-topic disruption, or deliberate misinformation.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
- Project communication spaces linked from this repository.
- Public spaces where someone is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through a contact channel listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not put private information in a public issue.

Include, when available:

- what happened,
- where it happened,
- who was involved,
- relevant links, logs, or screenshots.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Maintainers will review the available context, avoid conflicts of interest, and give the affected people a reasonable opportunity to respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, issue a private or public warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove someone from project spaces. The response will reflect the severity, impact, history, and context of the behavior.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Deliberately false or malicious reports may themselves violate this code of conduct.
